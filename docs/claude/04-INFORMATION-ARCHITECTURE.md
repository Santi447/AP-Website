## MVP Pages

For the first production version, prioritize:

1. Home
2. Services
3. Individual service pages
4. Projects/gallery
5. About
6. Contact/quote
7. Service area pages if service areas are confirmed

Blog/resources can be added later unless the owner wants content marketing immediately.

## Page Purposes

### Home Page

Purpose:

- Explain what A&P does.
- Separate residential and commercial/industrial work.
- Show trust signals.
- Push quote requests.
- Link to services and projects.

Recommended sections:

- Hero
- Services overview
- Residential work section
- Commercial/industrial section
- Why choose A&P
- Featured projects
- Quote CTA

### Services Page

Purpose:

- Give an overview of all major services.
- Link to individual service pages.
- Support SEO and user navigation.

### Individual Service Pages

Purpose:

- Rank for specific service keywords.
- Explain each service clearly.
- Show related project photos.
- Convert visitors into quote requests.

Each service page should include:

- H1
- Short intro
- Main image
- Detailed service explanation
- Benefits
- Process
- Related projects
- FAQs
- Quote CTA

### Projects Page

Purpose:

- Build trust with real photos.
- Let visitors see previous work.
- Let the owner add new projects through CMS.

Recommended features:

- Project grid
- Project type filters
- Featured projects
- Image gallery
- Related services

### About Page

Purpose:

- Make the business feel real and trustworthy.
- Explain who A&P is and what kind of jobs they handle.

### Contact / Quote Page

Purpose:

- Convert visitors into leads.
- Make calling or submitting a quote request easy.

### Service Area Pages

Purpose:

- Support local SEO.
- Explain that A&P serves specific areas.

Important: Do not create fake service area pages until the owner confirms the real target cities.
