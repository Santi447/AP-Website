# A&P Business Context

## Business Positioning

A&P should be positioned as a concrete pumping, placing, and finishing company that can support both residential and larger commercial/industrial jobs.

The website should make A&P feel reliable, practical, experienced, and easy to contact.

## Main Customer Types

### Residential Customers

Residential customers may need:

- Driveways
- Patios
- Garage pads
- Walkways
- Concrete prep
- Concrete placing
- Concrete finishing

They usually care about:

- Trust
- Quality
- Price clarity
- Past work
- Easy quotes
- Photos of previous jobs
- Local service availability

### Commercial and Industrial Customers

Commercial and industrial customers may need:

- Concrete placing crews
- Concrete finishing crews
- Pumping coordination
- Extra manpower
- Reliable jobsite support
- Larger pour support
- Subcontractor support

They usually care about:

- Reliability
- Crew availability
- Jobsite professionalism
- Ability to scale
- Safety awareness
- Previous similar work
- Fast communication

## Services to Represent

The website should support these service categories:

### Residential

- Driveway concrete
- Patio concrete
- Garage pad concrete
- Concrete prep work
- Concrete placing
- Concrete finishing

### Commercial

- Commercial concrete placing
- Commercial concrete finishing
- Concrete pumping coordination
- Jobsite manpower

### Industrial

- Industrial concrete placing
- Industrial concrete finishing
- Large-scale crew support
- Manpower support

## Tone of the Website

The content should sound:

- Professional
- Direct
- Local
- Trustworthy
- Clear
- Not overly corporate
- Not overly flashy

## Content Rules

Do not invent:

- Years in business
- Certifications
- Safety records
- Exact service areas
- Testimonials
- Project locations
- Phone numbers
- Business address

Use placeholders where owner confirmation is needed.
